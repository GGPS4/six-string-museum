Wing E

Tone Detective

Eight mystery tones. Each one is a guitar, a few pedals and an amp. Listen, then pick the rig you think made it. After each answer you'll see what made the sound, and you can load that rig on the pedalboard.

Wing F · Classroom

Guitar Teacher

Fifty short lessons in guitar theory, each teaching one new idea. Start at the musical alphabet and finish writing your own progression and solo. Every lesson has sounds to play and one question at the end.

Wing G

Chord Shape Tarot

Draw three cards: past, present and future. Each card is a chord with a meaning drawn from how it sounds. Then play the reading as a chord progression and find out which key your fortune is in.

Wing H

The Wood Library

Eleven tonewoods in labeled drawers. Open one to see its density, where it grows and what luthiers use it for, then hear it in the same guitar with nothing else changed. Densities are typical air-dried values; real boards vary.

Blind A/B test

Pick two woods. The same guitar plays the same riff twice. Can you hear the difference?

Wing I

Twenty notes. Find each one on the fretboard as fast as you can. A wrong fret adds two seconds. Pick one string to master or take on all six.

Wing J

The Guitar Family Tree

About 1,300 years of plucked strings, from the oud to the seven-string. Click any instrument to read about it and trace its line back to its ancestors. Dates are approximate.

Wing K

Broken Neck Hospital

Sick guitars come in with symptoms. Listen to the problem, make a diagnosis, then choose the treatment. Each case is worth two points: one for the diagnosis and one for the fix.
